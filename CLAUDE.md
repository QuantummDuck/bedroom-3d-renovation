# Bedroom 3D

Measured Blender replica of a real bedroom plus its renovation design (teak and navy), with a web planner and photoreal / CAD render pipelines.

**Start with `MARKETING_VIDEO_HANDOFF.md`**: where everything is, how each part works, how to change the look, and how to make videos. Baseline documentation is in `README.md`; the dated work log is `experiments/photoreal/WORKLOG.md`.

Rules:
- Never overwrite or delete earlier versions; new work goes into new files (`layout_vNN.py` writes `Bedroom_vNN_*.blend`). v19 is current.
- Units: centimetres in scripts, metres in Blender. Wall 3 is the slat wall, wall 4 the wardrobe, wall 2 the window, wall 1 the door.
- Do not add private data: no real location, no photos of the real room (`reference/`, `textures/photo/`), no copyrighted book-cover textures.
- Ask the owner before publishing, downloading or posting anything.
