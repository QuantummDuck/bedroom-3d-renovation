## A. Lighting

**What to buy for every strip:** 24 V **RGB+CCT (5-channel) COB** LED strip in an aluminium profile with an **opal diffuser**.
- RGB+CCT has dedicated warm-white chips, so each group can be a true 2000–3000 K white or any colour.
- COB gives a continuous line with no visible dots.
- Use one Wi-Fi/app controller per group, or a multi-zone controller, so each group has its own colour and dimmer.

## C. Round 5 notes from the new photos (no purchase needed)

- **Shelf spacing:** the shelves are about **26.5 cm apart** (top surfaces at about 144.5, 171 and 197.5 cm), not 23 cm as I'd estimated from the earlier photos. That gives 24 cm of clear height, so your 21–23 cm books stand with 1–3 cm to spare. The 1 cm shelf LED profile sits at the back edge, behind the books, so it doesn't get in their way.
- **Window:** the sun reaches your window from about **12:00 until sunset** all year (see `experiments/photoreal/Sun_Path_Diagram.png`). In the late afternoon it lands on the **wardrobe doors**, so pick a **satin or matt** teak finish there: gloss would bounce glare around the room.
